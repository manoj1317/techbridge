How TechBridge builds adviser trust

A clear review process helps learners connect with people who have relevant experience.

📄1. Background review

We review each adviser’s CV, experience, professional links and optional supporting documents before approval.

🛡️2. Approval before listing

Only approved profiles appear publicly. We may request more information or decline an application that cannot be verified.

⭐3. Learner feedback

After a completed consultation, learners can leave a review. Ratings and session history help build an honest reputation.

Future plan: introduce adviser subscriptions and payments only after consultation tracking, review moderation and clear payment rules are in place.
